## Editorial mapping

Every chapter uses one distinct image. The mapping follows the visible subject rather than the downloaded filename: village for the first hours, wilderness for exploration and stealth, ingredients for magic/crafting, a follower for companions, a home for Hearthfire, dark supernatural scenes for Dawnguard/Dragonborn, and the Creations/Dwemer material for long-term content management.
